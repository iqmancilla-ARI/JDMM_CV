# About Me

I am a Chemical Engineer with a professional background built at the intersection of industrial operations, engineering, continuous improvement and strategic execution.

Throughout my career, I have worked in environments where operational complexity, technical precision and decision-making under pressure are part of everyday business. This experience allowed me to develop a systems-oriented mindset focused not only on solving problems, but on understanding how organizations behave as interconnected systems.

My approach combines:
- engineering logic,
- operational discipline,
- analytical thinking,
- and organizational leadership.

I am deeply interested in how companies evolve when operational knowledge is integrated with technology, data and structured management methodologies.

Rather than viewing manufacturing, engineering or analytics as isolated disciplines, I understand them as components of the same operational architecture.

I believe operational excellence is not achieved through isolated improvements, but through the alignment of:
